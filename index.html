About

The Photographer

I'm Russell Haque — a hobbyist photographer based in Düsseldorf, with a passion for medium format film. I'm not one to debate the merits of film versus digital; both are beautiful in their own way, and I have deep respect for each.

What draws me to film is something harder to define — the slowness, the deep concentration, the genuine connection with the moment, and the hard work that goes into every single frame. That effort is what makes each photograph feel passionate and valuable to me. Nothing is accidental. Everything is earned.
